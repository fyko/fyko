# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 15, 2026

This policy explains what information Fyko Authentik ("the Service", "we", "us") collects when you use the sign-in service at idp.fyko.net, and what we do with it. The Service is run by Carter Himmel, an individual based in Austin, Texas, USA. It is a personal, non-commercial project.

The Service is a single sign-on (SSO) provider. You use it to log in to other apps and websites ("Connected Apps"). Each Connected App has its own privacy policy, and this one does not cover what they do with your data.

## What we collect

**Information you give us**
- Username, display name and email address
- Your password, which we store only as a one-way hash and never in plain text
- Multi-factor authentication details, such as authenticator app secrets, security key / passkey public keys, or recovery codes

**Information from social logins**
If you sign in with an outside account (for example Google, GitHub or Discord), we receive what that provider shares, usually your account ID, username, email address, name and profile picture. We store the link between that account and yours so you can sign in with it again.

**Information collected automatically**
- **Sessions:** your IP address, browser/device (user agent), and when the session was created and last used
- **Security and audit logs:** sign-ins, failed sign-in attempts, password and MFA changes, app authorizations, and other account events, together with the IP address and the approximate location (country/city) and network provider derived from it
- **Consents:** which Connected Apps you have authorized and what you allowed them to see

**Cookies**
We only use cookies the Service needs to work: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and a security (CSRF) cookie. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not track you across other sites.

## How we use it

- To sign you in and keep your account secure
- To share your identity with a Connected App when you choose to sign in to it
- To detect and prevent abuse, fraud, brute-force attacks and unauthorized access
- To fix problems with the Service
- To contact you about your account or important changes to the Service

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or build marketing profiles.

## Who we share it with

- **Connected Apps you sign in to.** When you sign in to an app, it receives the information it asks for and you allow, usually your user ID, username, name, email address and group memberships.
- **Social login providers.** If you use a social login, that provider knows you used it to sign in here.
- **Infrastructure providers** that host or deliver the Service (such as server hosting, DNS and email delivery). They process data only to run the Service.
- **Avatar services.** If enabled, your profile picture may be loaded from Gravatar, which receives a hash of your email address.
- **When the law requires it**, or when it is needed to protect the Service, its users or the public.

## How long we keep it

- **Account information** is kept until your account is deleted.
- **Sessions** expire on their own and are then deleted.
- **Security and audit logs** are kept for up to {{LOG_RETENTION}} and then deleted automatically.
- Backups may keep deleted data for a short time until they are replaced.

## Your choices and rights

You can:
- See and update most of your information in your account settings
- Remove social logins, MFA devices and app authorizations
- End your active sessions
- Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account by emailing {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}

We give these rights to everyone, wherever you live, and we will reply within 30 days. Deleting your account here does not delete data that Connected Apps already have. Ask them directly.

## Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords and optional multi-factor authentication. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that your information will never be accessed without permission. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you as required by law.

## Children

The Service is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## International users

The Service is run from the United States. If you use it from another country, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

## Changes

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant, we will notify you by email or on the sign-in page before they take effect. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
